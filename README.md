# ⚡ EcoOrbit — Space-Based Green Compute Engine
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Team EcoOrbit**

EcoOrbit is an interactive Streamlit simulation engine that models a hybrid Earth–Low Earth Orbit (LEO) compute architecture. By shifting energy-intensive AI batch training jobs off strained terrestrial grids and into Mid-LEO (500–600 km) using 24/7 solar power and passive vacuum radiative cooling, EcoOrbit provides a sustainable pathway for scaling artificial intelligence without depleting Earth's power grids and freshwater resources.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Hybrid Compute Optimization:** Dynamically models workloads between low-latency terrestrial infrastructure and zero-emission orbital satellite clusters.
- **Environmental Impact Metrics:** Quantifies annual CO₂ emissions avoided (`4,750.0 MT/yr`) and freshwater saved (`168,000 L/yr`) from eliminating terrestrial HVAC cooling.
- **Orbital Debris Guardrails:** Integrates NORAD catalog data and evaluates the Kessler Risk Index (`40.5 / 100`) to ensure sustainable orbital shell density.
- **Multilingual Support:** Fully accessible simulation interface in English, Spanish, and French.

## 📌 NASA & Open Data Sources
- **NASA POWER Project:** Solar irradiance and meteorological data in Low Earth Orbit (`https://power.larc.nasa.gov/`)
- **NASA Orbital Debris Program Office (ODPO):** Orbital shell density and debris collision risk modeling (`https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/`)
- **EPA eGRID:** U.S. terrestrial power grid carbon intensity baselines (`https://www.epa.gov/egrid`)
- **CelesTrak:** Live active satellite orbital telemetry (`https://celestrak.org/`)
